# Render QA

QA runs automatically after every review copy and final render (queued for the person who asked for the render;
`metarush render submit --no-qa` skips it). A review copy MetaRush makes by itself to follow a cut's latest version
 is checked the same way, for the person who saved that version; its check rings the bell only when it finds
something. `metarush qa run <render-or-revision> --wait` decodes a **finished render** (the actual MP4, fetched through the
verified cache) and checks it against the RenderPlan it was made from. `metarush qa show <report|render|revision>`
prints the latest report. The `video-qa` skill runs it, Reads the images and proposes fixes.

```text
qa.run (op) → job "qa" (any worker; inline with --wait when none is online)
  → resolve the same plan the render used (revision spec + render preset + transcripts)
  → ffprobe (+ packet count) → one ffmpeg pass: blackdetect, freezedetect, silencedetect, ebur128
  → classify against the plan and the source evidence → layout heuristics
  → what's on screen: the footage's faces and text (on-screen index) through each clip's crop, against the captions
    and graphics; on a Mac the rendered frames read with the on-screen helper
  → contact sheet + moments sheet + stills → media store (content-addressed) → qa_reports + render.qa summary
```

## Findings
Each finding has `severity` (error | warning | info), `kind`, a timeline range and `timecode`, `basis`
(`observed` = measured on the decoded file; `heuristic` = inferred from plan geometry or conventions), a message,
the clips/overlays involved, frame IDs showing it, and — where possible — a `fix` with a summary and suggested
edit ops written against the rendered revision. Ops are hints: write them into a plan and run `edit check`.
Verdict: `fail` with any error, `warnings` with any warning, else `pass`.

## Checks and thresholds (QA version `qa-7`)
| Check | Basis | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| conformance | observed | frame count within ±1 of `durationInFrames`; size within ±2 px of the plan × render scale (review = 0.5); fps equal; H.264 and yuv420p expected (warning otherwise); audio stream present when the plan has audible clips or audio tracks (error); audio/video length within 0.2 s; rotation flag; VFR |
| black_frames | observed | `blackdetect pix_th=0.10 pic_th=0.98`. Planned dark spans (fade-in from the background, dip-to-black = previous clip's fade-out + incoming fade-in, clip fade-outs, full-frame cards such as `endcard.cta`; a fade through white is static but not dark), widened by 2 frames, are intentional and only recorded under `measurements.black` with what explains them. Black that the source evidence also shows → `source_black` warning with trim/split ops. Anything else: fewer than 3 frames = `flash_frame` warning, more = `black_frames` error |
| frozen_frames | observed | `freezedetect n=-60dB d=1.0`. Planned cards/dips and black spans excluded; static in the source evidence → `static_source` info; otherwise a warning, or an error when the source evidence shows motion there and the freeze lasts ≥ 2 s (render stalled / repeated frames) |
| loudness | observed | EBU R128 integrated loudness and true peak. Target = output platform; when that is `generic`, the first platform in the project brief. True peak > 0 dBTP = clipping error; above the −1 dBTP convention = warning; integrated outside ±2 LU of the target = warning with clip-volume / audio-gain ops (limited by peak headroom; MetaRush has no limiter). Silent track when audio is planned = error  Cuts whose mixer sets the overall level (narration or a mix section) get mix fixes instead of per-clip gains: a loudness target or peak ceiling (`setMix`), or — when the narration's peaks set the limit and the parts without narration are quiet — the music level where nobody talks (about 4 LU under the target); a raw recording gets "clean up the narration". |
| dialogue_silence | observed | `silencedetect -50dB ≥0.3s` against speech spans: word spans from transcript captions resolved with the same timing code as the render (or segment-level dialogue ranges). Flags silence ≥ 0.75 s inside word-timed speech (≥ 1.2 s with segment timing) or ≥ 80 % of a speech span |
| layout | heuristic | Approximate template boxes (mirroring `video/src/core/layout.ts` and template metrics; a project motion graphic by the region its check recorded: the lower 26% of the content box, a centered box or the full frame) and the two-line caption band: captions stacked with a lower third or center card (`caption_overlay_stack`), captions under a full-frame card (`captions_hidden`), overlapping overlays, caption lines on screen < 0.25 s (`caption_flash`), > 25 characters/s (`caption_fast`, info), an end card that stops less than a second before the video ends (`endcard_gap`: the last shot flashes back after the call to action) |
| safe_area | heuristic | Vertical render with non-social margins: warning when the project brief targets Shorts/TikTok/Reels (or captions/graphics would sit under that app's UI), info otherwise; suggests `setOutput` with the platform |
| on_screen | observed on a Mac, else heuristic | [visual-index.md](visual-index.md#whats-on-screen-in-renders): every 0.5 s of single-clip picture, the footage's faces and text from its on-screen index placed through the clip's crop exactly as the renderer places it (`editspec.CropRect`, paths that follow movement included): `face_cut_off` (the moment's main face < 80% in the picture; fix: `updateClip` `framing.auto: follow`, or `fit` when it already follows), `faces_covered` (≥ 25% of a face under the caption band while a caption shows; fix: `setCaptions` to the band that stays clear), `graphic_covers_face` (≥ 25% under a graphic that isn't full screen), `text_under_captions` (≥ 30% of footage text under the caption band; fix: `setCaptions`). Stretches under 0.75 s are dropped. On a Mac up to 40 rendered frames are read with the on-screen helper: a face at the very edge, words over a face, or the footage's own words in the caption band make the finding `observed`. Skipped (with why) when no clip's footage has an index; conversation clips and nested cuts are left out. |
| narration | heuristic | Cuts with a voiceover, from the plan's gain envelopes and measured source loudness: `narration_early` (a line plays more than 2 frames before its anchor), `narration_over_dialogue` (narration over on-camera speech for 0.6 s or more, even when that speech is turned down), `narration_masked` (music or clip sound less than 10 LU under the voice), `narration_clipping` (narration peaks above −0.5 dBTP after its gain, or a recording whose own peaks reach −0.1 dBFS), `narration_script_mismatch` (a recording contains less than 85 % of its line's words) |
| transitions | heuristic | From the cut report Go resolved for the rendered spec (`editspec.CutReport`, the same one `edit check` warns from): `split_too_short` (a split edit the footage or the neighbouring shots could not give its full length: "Sam's clip has only 0.2 s of sound before it starts, so Sam's voice starts 0.2 s early instead of 0.4 s"; fix: `setTransition` with what fits), `split_cuts_word` (a sound change that cuts a word: still sounding when its clip's sound has faded out, or already under way when it starts; fix: sound `auto`), `split_drops_words` (words a clip includes that a split leaves unheard), `voices_overlap` (two people heard at once during a dissolve-like transition or a blend of 0.15 s or more; fix: keep the picture, sound `auto`), `transition_shortened` (info: an overlapping transition shorter than asked), `flash_shot` (a shot seen for 2 frames or fewer; fix: `removeClip`), `short_shot` (info: under 0.4 s), `sound_blip` (a clip's sound heard for under 0.25 s). Words spoken by the other clip's person (caught by both microphones) are heard through that clip and never count. |
| cut_loudness | observed | The render's own momentary loudness (ebur128, every 0.1 s): at each cut between two clips with speech, the gated loudness of each clip's sound where only it plays (the outgoing one up to the sound change, the incoming one after the blend, within 4 s of the cut). A jump of 6 LU or more is `loudness_jump`; fix: turn on `mix.levelSpeech` (`setMix`), or, when it is on, turn the louder clip down (`updateClip` volume). The measured values are kept in `measurements.cutLoudness`. |
| speech_continuity | observed | The render's audio is transcribed locally and aligned with the words the plan expects (clip transcripts inside each clip's range, narration take words or the script; words more than 1.5 s apart are never matched). `speech_repeated`, `speech_missing`, `speech_unexpected` with a timecode; timing jumps (a slice played twice or skipped) shift everything after them and are reported too. Fillers and single transcription slips are ignored; a repeated or dropped phrase (or a whole line) and jumps of 1 s or more are errors. Skipped without a local transcriber, without word-timed speech in the plan, or with `qa run --no-speech-check`. |
| clearer_voice | heuristic | Information, not a defect: each voice the render plays made clearer (a person, or the narration), with what that did in plain words, from the measurements taken when its clearer copy was made: the voice clarity score before and after, the background level, words studio put back ([sound.md](sound.md)). |
| conversation_voices | observed | Conversation clips ([conversations.md](conversations.md)): each person's loudness in the render over the words only they say (at least 1.5 s; the render's momentary loudness, gated like `cut_loudness`) against the loudest person's. 6 dB or more quieter = `conversation_quiet` warning, 20 dB or more = `conversation_inaudible` error; fix: turn that person up (`setMix` with their `mix.voices` level). A person whose microphone the clip leaves out while they talk ≥ 3 s = `conversation_muted` (info; not for question cards). Measured values are kept in `measurements.conversations`. |
| conversation_picture | heuristic | From the plan's shots: one person alone on screen longer than the conversation style's limit (podcast 30 s, interview 45 s, panel 30 s, reactions 12 s, b-roll 40 s; 30 s when no style is known) = `conversation_one_sided` (fix: everyone together for a few seconds in the middle); someone talking that long unseen = `conversation_unseen` (fix: a shot of them); someone doing ≥ 20 % of the talking but on screen less than half that share = `conversation_balance`. An off-camera interviewer (interview style) is exempt. |
| caption_words | heuristic | [transcripts.md](transcripts.md): the rendered version's caption words against the transcripts as they are now. A word fixed since the version was made = `caption_fixed` warning ("The captions say “Maplegrove” where the transcript now says “Maple Grove” (at 0:01). Make a new version to use the fixed words."; caption words over the same changed words are one note); a word the transcriber gave less than 0.5 = `caption_unsure` (info); a capitalized word that doesn't start a sentence and is on no spelling list and no person's name = `caption_name` (info: "“Elmwood” appears 4 times in the captions. Is it spelled right?"). Passes with "The captions have every fix made to the transcript." Skipped when the version shows no captions. The same list, live, is `captions check`. |
| call_footage | heuristic | [call-recordings.md](call-recordings.md), for footage whose picture switches were found to the frame: `edge_flash` (a split-second of another picture at a clip's start or end, at most 0.75 s and at least a frame of it in the render: "Clip 3, Lena's answer, opens with a split-second of Maya (0.2 s)"; warning, an error when the project's rules say no flashes; fix: `trimClip` at the exact switch, with the sound starting earlier or carrying on when that would cut words — `cut flashes` does every one at once), `printed_names` (names a call app printed in the picture that the render shows uncovered; info; fix: the `name.bar` graphic for the whole video, `cut names`), `name_bar_mismatch` (a name bar naming someone else than who is on screen for more than half a second; fix: write the names again with `cut names`). Conversation clips and nested cuts are checked too (their own shots and cuts, and the footage inside them). |
| project_rules | heuristic | [keeping.md](keeping.md#project-rules-and-checks-and-house-rules): the project's rules that MetaRush can measure — `captions-top` / `captions-bottom` (the version's captions must sit there; fix: `setCaptions`), `first-names` (names in name bars and lower thirds must be one word; fix: the first word), `no-flashes` (`edge_flash` findings become errors), `max-length:<s>` (fix: shorten it by the difference), `shape:<landscape|vertical|square>` (fix: `setOutput`); `rule_broken` warnings say which rule and how. Rules it can't measure are listed once as `rule_by_eye` (info: for a person to look at). A rule using one of the promises' measures (below) is measured too, as a warning. Promises are left to `promises`. |
| promises | observed, or estimated from the plan per measure | [ship-gate.md](ship-gate.md#promises-made-to-the-client): every rule or house rule marked `promise`, measured with a plain sentence and a result in `promises[]` (`QAPromise`: words, measure, `says`, `status` kept / broken / unmeasured / notApplicable, `measured`, value, limit, unit, basis, the finding's ID). `max-length:<s>[@shape]` (observed: the decoded length, within a frame; another shape: doesn't apply), `shape:`, `captions-top|bottom`, `first-names`, `no-flashes`, `captions` (on over ≥ 90% of the speech, letters ≥ 3.5% of the picture's shorter side from the look's own numbers, no line of 0.25 s or more faster than 25 letters a second), `logo-end:<s>` (a graphic with a logo field, or an end card, through the last seconds) and `logo-start:<s>` (within the first, at least a second), `speech-over-music:<steps>` (wherever someone speaks over music, each recording's measured loudness plus its gain in the mix, averaged per half second; the smallest gap counts, half a step of slack; fix: the music bus down), `disclosed` (every generated clip, AI voice — each change of spoken words its own part, in its speaker's voice — and AI picture told about by a graphic whose words say so while it plays, or by the version's description; the description's fingerprint goes in `notesSha`), `faces-in-frame` (qa-5's `face_cut_off`; unmeasured without the on-screen check), `min-size:<px>` (the decoded shorter side; a review copy: the cut's own size), `min-fps:<n>` (decoded). A broken promise is `promise_broken` (an **error**: the version waits before it goes out) with the fix as edit ops where one exists; one that can't be measured here is `promise_unmeasured` (a warning, never a hold). |
| conversation_glitches | observed | A member's frozen webcam picture (their recording's own freeze evidence, ≥ 0.4 s) where it is on screen = `conversation_freeze` (fix: show someone else over it, then go back); a sound dropout clean-up found where that person is heard = `conversation_dropout` (not when someone chose to keep it); a recording lined up unsurely and never nudged = `conversation_sync`. Frozen frames the render shows there count as explained under `frozen_frames`. |

### Loudness conventions
| Platform | Integrated | True peak |
|---|---|---|
| shorts, tiktok, reels, youtube | ≈ −14 LUFS (±2 LU) | ≤ −1 dBTP |
| generic | ≈ −16 LUFS (±2 LU) | ≤ −1 dBTP |

These are common delivery conventions, not platform guarantees; platforms normalize playback loudness themselves.

## Visual evidence
- `contact-sheet.jpg`: 8–24 evenly spaced frames labeled `#n`, timeline `M:SS.mmm` and the clip on screen.
- `moments-sheet.jpg`: a frame just after every cut (after a dip, fade, wipe, slide, push, zoom or whip has finished;
  half-way through a dissolve), 0.6 s into every overlay,
  at the middle of every finding, plus the first and last frame; the plan safe area is outlined in amber and the
  caption band in cyan while a caption is on screen. At most 48 moments (cuts are sampled beyond that).
- `frames/NN-<reason>-<M>m<SS.mmm>.jpg`: each moment at 960 px on the long side.

They are written to `<scratch>/qa/<report-id>/` on the machine that ran QA and published to the media store;
`qa show` (CLI) copies them into the same folder on any other machine and prints the paths.

## Limitations
- Black/freeze detection cannot tell whether an *unplanned* dark or static shot is artistic; source evidence
  (`footage analyze`) helps attribute it.
- Music or room tone under a dialogue dropout hides it from the silence check; transcripts can be wrong.
- Layout checks are geometric approximations; the on-screen check knows where faces and footage text are only at the
  index's precision (about a frame a second) and only for footage a Mac looked at — legibility and framing still need
  someone to look at the frames (Claude Reads them in the `video-qa` skill). The `qa-zones` render self-test keeps the
  caption band and the template boxes honest against real renders.
- Review copies are half resolution; check the final render before delivery.
- Transition and split checks rely on transcript word timings (± a few tens of ms; MetaRush keeps 30 ms before and
  60 ms after every word clear) and on speaker names to know whose words a clip carries; unnamed voices are only
  compared within one recording.
- The speech check is only as good as local transcription: names, music under speech and overlapping voices can
  produce false "missing" or "unexpected" words (single slips are ignored); it does not judge pronunciation or tone.
- Narration level checks are estimates from the plan and measured source loudness; the loudness check above measures
  the render itself.
